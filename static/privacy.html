FoxxeEye Privacy Notice

FoxxeEye is a diagnostic tool that shows you what information your device naturally exposes when it connects to a website. The point is to teach you how the web sees you — not to identify you beyond what your browser already broadcasts.

1. What Data We Process

When you load FoxxeEye, our server receives the same basic request data that any web server receives:

  • Your IP address (used to look up geolocation + ISP)
  • Your browser’s User-Agent string
  • Your accepted languages and basic request headers
  • Optional fingerprinting details calculated in your browser

2. Why We Process It

The entire purpose of FoxxeEye is to show you exactly what a normal website learns about you the moment you visit it. Nothing more.

3. What We Don’t Collect

  • No names, accounts, identifiers, or login data
  • No cookies beyond a temporary session
  • No behavioural tracking
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party scripts
  • No long-term logs unless explicitly enabled by you

4. Storage & Retention

By default, request data is held in-memory only for the duration of your session and is not stored permanently. If logging is temporarily enabled for development or debugging, it is kept locally on our server and never shared.

5. Legal Basis (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, the minimal data processing performed by FoxxeEye is covered under legitimate interest: demonstrating how HTTP requests work and enabling the tool to function. No data is used to identify or profile you.

6. Your Rights

If you want to know whether any diagnostic logs exist for your session, or you want them deleted, you can contact the administrator of this instance of FoxxeEye. For the typical public version, nothing is stored at all.

7. Contact

For questions, concerns, or data requests, contact the operator of this FoxxeEye deployment. If you are running this on your own server, that’s you.
